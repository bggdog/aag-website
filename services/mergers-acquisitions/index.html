Why ops drive the number

Four reasons this work is worth the runway

You are busy and skeptical, and you should be. Here is the honest case for starting before the LOI.

Findings cost more than prep

A diligence surprise rarely stays a paperwork issue. It becomes a holdback, a price chip, or a deal that stalls while you scramble to explain it.

Buyers buy systems, not heroes

If the company only works because you carry the schedule, the payroll, and the payer calls, the multiple reflects that risk.

Clean cash flow is underwritable

Revenue that depends on undocumented workarounds or aging AR is discounted. Clean collections and clear reporting are what underwriters trust.

The runway is the advantage

Twelve to thirty-six months is enough time to fix the hard things without panic. Waiting until a buyer asks for files is how you pay for haste.
